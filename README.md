## What is [Quich](https://quich.shop) 
Quich is a web platform tool built to aid whatsapp vendor who need much more than just a number

## Why did you create Quich?
- *To aid whatsapp vendor that lack technical skill to operate their online.*
- *To provide online access to vendor store rather than sharing phone contact.*
- *To ease ordering, `no checkout payment yet`, but order is made to vendor in a style format directly from their store.*
- *Track orders and manage product on the dashboard.*

##  Revenue Generation (*How do get paid?*)
Revenue is generated via subscriptions.

#### Starter plan > NGN2200 (month) > 22k(1yr)
The starter plan is targeted toward small business with small inventory, but order made by customers are unlimited.

*`Features includes`*
- Storefront link
- Upload up to 20 products.
- Unlimited direct orders.
- Add your Community/Group link
- Community & Email Support
            

#### Premium plan > NGN4000 (Month) > 40k(1yr)
The Premium is targeted toward large businesses with a lot of inventories, plan who good for vendors that are wholesalers.

*`Features includes`*
- Everything in Starter plan
- Upload up to 100 products.
            

## Tool and Framework used in creating quich
- **Vercel**
- **Firebase**
- **Whatsapp Api**
- **Paystack**
- **NextJs | ViteJs | NodeJS**

#### Vercel
Vercel is where these projects where hosted but it limited, later all the project will be moved to a VPS instead.

#### Firebase
Firebase is the backend software for quich: `Database`, `Authentication`, `Storage`, `FCM & Analytics`. Firebase help with this while i focus on the user, marketing and other things.

#### Whatsapp API
For now this is just for send OTP only.


#### Paystack
For collecting subscriptions and managing subscription for user

#### NextJs
NextJs was chosen cos of its ssr functionality, and other things.
